Common questions

Do I need to sacrifice productive land?

Strips are typically planted on the least productive margins — slopes, wet hollows, field edges — where yield is already compromised by erosion.

How much does planting cost?

Establishment cost is typically €1,500–2,500/ha depending on rhizome source and machinery. This is largely offset by EU agri-environment payments in most regions.

When can I expect a return?

First commercial harvest is typically year two or three. Most strips are cash-positive by year four, including establishment cost recovery.
