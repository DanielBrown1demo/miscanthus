The Solution

Meet your new
field boundary

Miscanthus — a perennial grass native to Asia — grows up to 4 metres tall, establishes dense root systems within two seasons, and produces a substantial annual biomass harvest. Plant it in strips along your field margins and it quietly goes to work.

Miscanthus × giganteus

The sterile hybrid that grows up to 4 m, produces 10–25 t/ha of dry biomass annually, and spreads exclusively by rhizome — no invasive risk. Established once, it lasts 20+ years.

Deep Root Architecture

Root systems reach 2–3 m deep within 3 seasons, holding soil in place even during extreme rainfall events. The dense underground mat is the primary barrier against slope erosion.

Annual Harvest Cycle

Cut in late winter when moisture content is lowest. The above-ground biomass is ready for pellets, biogas, combustion, or sale. The plant regrows from the same rhizome next spring.

How It Works

Three steps to
protected, productive fields

1

Plan Your Stripes

We assess your field topography, erosion risk zones, and prevailing water runoff paths. Together we design strip placement to intercept sediment flow while minimising land sacrifice.

2

Plant Once

Rhizomes are planted mechanically in spring. Within the first season you'll see 1–2 m growth. By year two, the strip is fully established and the root barrier is actively working.

3

Harvest Every Year

From year two onward, cut and collect. Use the biomass yourself for heating, or sell to a growing network of biomass buyers. The same plants keep working and keep paying for 20+ years.

Why It Works

Good for your land.
Good for your bottom line.

Environmental

For the Land

  • Stops topsoil loss — dense roots lock soil particles in place during rain events
  • Filters runoff — the strip intercepts sediment and nutrients before they reach watercourses
  • Builds carbon — Miscanthus sequesters significant carbon in the soil rhizome system each year
  • Boosts biodiversity — overwintering stems provide habitat for insects and birds
  • Reduces chemical inputs — healthier soil margins mean less need for soil conditioners
  • Near-zero inputs — no irrigation, no fertiliser, no pesticides after establishment

Economic

For the Farmer

  • Annual biomass income — 10–25 dry tonnes per hectare of strip, every year
  • Multiple end markets — heating pellets, biogas, straw board, or direct sale
  • EU agri-environment payments — eligible for greening subsidies and CAP eco-schemes
  • 20-year productive life — plant once, earn for two decades from the same rhizomes
  • Protects crop yield — soil protection directly maintains the productivity of your main crop
  • Hedge against regulation — proactive compliance with tightening erosion legislation
